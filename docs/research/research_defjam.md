## 1. Basic facts

- **Release date (NA, all platforms incl. Xbox):** September 21, 2004. EU October 1, 2004; AU September 24, 2004. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Def_Jam:_Fight_for_NY)
- **Developer:** AKI Corporation (with EA Canada). **Publisher:** EA Games. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Def_Jam:_Fight_for_NY)
- **Engine lineage:** Built on AKI Corporation's proprietary wrestling engine — the same engine family behind N64's *WWF No Mercy* / *WrestleMania 2000* / *WCW* titles. *Def Jam Vendetta* (2003, PS2/GC/Xbox) used the AKI engine "largely unmodified"; *Fight for NY* is the same lineage further modified for the brawler/street-fighting aesthetic and is a direct narrative/mechanical sequel to Vendetta. [Wikipedia — Def Jam Vendetta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Def_Jam_Vendetta) [NeoGAF thread on AKI history](https://www.neogaf.com/threads/what-happened-to-aki-corp.1120094/)
- **AKI → syn Sophia:** AKI Corporation was renamed syn Sophia, Inc. on April 1, 2007, and moved away from wrestling/fighting games (later known for *Style Savvy*). [Wikipedia — Syn Sophia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syn_Sophia)
- **Xbox Title ID:** `45410049` (hex, used by xemu's title-based compat DB) / catalog code **EA-073**, version **1.01**. XBE certificate Title ID `1161887817` (decimal form of the same ID) confirmed via xemu compat page. [xemu.app title page](https://xemu.app/titles/45410049/) [Cxbx-Reloaded compat issue #852](https://github.com/Cxbx-Reloaded/game-compatibility/issues/852)
- **Redump naming:** Listed as **"Def Jam - Fight for NY (USA)"** on Redump. [Redump forum thread](http://forum.redump.org/viewtopic.php?pid=129401) (fetch failed at time of research; treat exact disc-size figures as **UNVERIFIED**).
- **Disc size (full ISO vs. XISO):** Not independently confirmed in this session — the Redump forum page (which would list exact byte size/redump hash) could not be fetched (ECONNREFUSED). The general Original-Xbox pattern of "Redump raw ISO ~6.8–8 GB vs. stripped XISO ~ a few GB depending on used disc space" is a known community convention, but the specific figures for this title are **UNVERIFIED** — recommend pulling the actual redump.org datfile before relying on this for build planning.
- **Single XBE / sections:** Not directly confirmed by any fetched source. Original Xbox titles are near-universally a single `default.xbe` with internal sections (no loader-visible "DLL" style split); nothing found suggests FFNY deviates from this norm, but no source explicitly states "single XBE" for this title — **UNVERIFIED**, worth confirming by inspecting the XBE header yourself once you have the dump.

## 3. Emulator compatibility

### xemu (Original Xbox emulator)
- **Compat page:** https://xemu.app/titles/45410049/ — **Status: "Starts" (with significant issues)**, last tested Sept 11 2026 against xemu 0.8.136-47-g75650bd8cd.
- **Primary blocker:** a **"PTimer unsupported"** error/warning causes frequent freezing; game reportedly reaches an in-game state but crashes/freezes often enough to be "unplayable for now" per the most recent report. [xemu.app/titles/45410049](https://xemu.app/titles/45410049/)
- **GitHub issue #1124** (xemu-project/xemu): documents the PTimer-unsupported freeze specifically for this game — an unimplemented PTimer (Xbox NV2A/kernel performance-timer-adjacent) command halts progress past the loading screen on older xemu builds (issue opened against xemu 0.7.55). The issue as fetched did not show a definitive resolution/fix comment, though the compat page (tested against a much newer build) shows the game now gets further ("Starts") — suggesting partial fixes landed since, but freezing persists. A community-modified/custom xemu build was cited in a YouTube video as needed to get further than mainline at some point. [github.com/xemu-project/xemu/issues/1124](https://github.com/xemu-project/xemu/issues/1124) [YouTube "Def Jam Fight for NY XEMU Custom Build"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iahm6QU_AMI)
- **Implication for your project:** PTimer is a low-level Xbox hardware/kernel timing primitive; the game evidently polls or depends on it in a way most Xbox titles don't stress as heavily — worth flagging as a specific HLE/kernel-timing edge case to get right in your recomp, since even a mature emulator (xemu) still has open issues here.

### Cxbx-Reloaded
- **Compat issue #852** (`Cxbx-Reloaded/game-compatibility`, EA-073, v1.01): status label = "Shows splash screens, logos, intros, etc and/or has audible effects, but doesn't progress further" — i.e. **hangs indefinitely on the loading screen**, never reaches gameplay. XDK version recorded as **5849**. A note flags "XBE appears tampered with" in the submitted dump (may reflect the reporter's specific ISO/XBE rather than the retail original — worth independently verifying against a clean redump). Regions logged: NTSC-U (Region 1), PAL (Region 4), Japan (Region 2) — interesting since Wikipedia/other sources only mention NA/EU/AU release; a Japan region flag here is **UNVERIFIED**/possibly a mislabel and should be checked separately. [github.com/Cxbx-Reloaded/game-compatibility/issues/852](https://github.com/Cxbx-Reloaded/game-compatibility/issues/852)
- The Cxbx-Reloaded game-compatibility repo was **archived (read-only) July 27, 2021** — so this compat record is a frozen historical snapshot, not actively maintained. No newer Cxbx-Reloaded-specific data was found in this session.
- **Implication:** Cxbx-Reloaded (which does more direct x86 passthrough / HLE of D3D8 than xemu's full HW emulation) can't even get past the loading screen, while xemu (closer to full LLE) gets further. This suggests the game leans on low-level kernel/timing/D3D8 behavior that's hard to HLE correctly — a caution flag for a static-recomp approach that will also rely heavily on HLE'd kernel and D3D8 calls.
