# v1.2.0 — Fighter Mod Support, voice subtitles and Thunderstore mods

The first stable release with **Fighter Mod Support**, tested through three betas. It also
adds subtitles for the game's spoken lines (with a language mod that provides them), links
the launcher's mods to their Thunderstore pages, and fixes profiles disappearing after a long
Story session.

You need your own supported USA Xbox dump. The game-free app verifies it and compiles locally.
No game executable or game data is distributed. Fighter packs are not included.

## What is new since v1.1.2

### Custom fighters

- **Install fighter packs in Mods → Fighters**: drop the ZIP on the launcher or Browse for it,
  then enable it. Packs add fighters next to the retail roster and other packs, with their own
  selector art, names, loading and results screens, voices, supported moves and cinematics.
- **Up to three outfits per fighter**, chosen in the game's own clothing selector. Created
  fighters (CAF) and fighters built from a retail fighter both support it; outfits share
  progress and combat settings and keep their own art.
- **Final battle attributes and Charisma** for created fighters: a pack can set Upper/Lower
  power, Speed, Toughness, Health and Charisma directly (0–50, as shown in the game's menus)
  instead of the five training values.
- **Team (four-player) selection** works with custom fighters, which could leave the screen
  blank and unresponsive before, and packs can bring their own tall Team portrait and
  available/picked icons per outfit.
- **Face sculpts with neck-normal corrections** from the Fighter Workshop are applied in game.
- **HD textures for created fighters**, up to 2048x2048 per outfit, stored losslessly so a
  three-outfit HD fighter fits; each outfit's selector titlecard shows that outfit.
- **Authored entrance cinematics** for created fighters: a pack can bring its own timed entrance
  performance, mouth track and intro voice next to the retail performance choices.
- Saves stay in `save/` for everyone; custom-fighter unlocks are kept beside them in
  `fighter-state/` by pack and fighter. Each launch keeps rolling backups in `save-backups/`.
- Packs are compiled when you enable, disable or update them, so Play starts at once; an
  unchanged set reuses its verified build (a 46-fighter roster restarts in seconds). A pack
  that fails its checks is left out and the game starts without it, keeping your saves.
- Fighter numbers and roster order no longer depend on the order packs were installed, and a
  fighter set digest identifies a roster (groundwork for online forks).
- Make packs with **[Fighter Workshop 0.3.0](https://github.com/vyanhursky/defjam-ffny-fighter-workshop/releases/tag/v0.3.0)**,
  released alongside. Its exports need this game version for the features above; the game
  tells the Workshop which features it supports, and the Workshop can preview retail fighters'
  animations and build outfits from them.

### Language and subtitles

- **Voice subtitles.** A language mod may include subtitles for fighter intros and victory
  lines, shops, the gym tutorial, taunts and voicemail. They are on when the active language
  mod has them (**Interface → Language and subtitles**, `[language] voice_subtitles`).
- **Portuguese (Brazil) translation** is in the launcher's Mods page: SanVG's translation,
  hosted with permission by Joao13, adapted by jjalles-dev, with voice subtitles.
- **Fighter packs work with a translation active** (ZARF's Spanish, Portuguese); before,
  the betas refused every fighter pack while one was on (#284).

### Mods and Thunderstore

- The project's mods are downloaded from its
  [Thunderstore community](https://thunderstore.io/c/def-jam-fight-for-ny-recompiled/); the
  signed catalogue still decides what is offered and checks every file.
- **View on Thunderstore** beside each catalogue mod, and **Open the Thunderstore community**
  under the list.

### Fixes

- **Profiles no longer vanish after about 70 saves in one session** (#271). The save was never
  lost, but the profile list came back empty until the game was restarted.
- **Setup, update and repair no longer stop with "Unexpected files in setup payload"** when
  Python left `__pycache__` folders in the installation (#263); the error now names the files.
- The **UPDATE GAME** button on "Finish your update" is no longer cut off at the Steam Deck's
  1280x800.
- From the betas: closing the window during a save waits for it; fatal errors show a message
  (and on the launcher's Home page) on macOS and Linux too; data folders with accented or
  non-Latin names work on Windows; more than 36 custom songs no longer crash (v1.1.2).

## Downloads and updating

- **Windows x64:** `DefJamRecompiled-1.2.0-windows-x64.exe`.
- **macOS, Apple Silicon:** `DefJamRecompiled-1.2.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
- **Linux / Steam Deck:** `DefJamRecompiled-1.2.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`.

Each package includes its SHA-256 and provenance. Update through the app, or download the new
app and open it. Saves, settings, mods, songs and the verified dump are kept. Coming from a
1.2.0 beta with fighter packs, the first start rebuilds the packs before the window opens;
this can take a few minutes.

## Testing and limits

On Windows the release candidate passed the full regression, **17 of 17**
applicable checks (four macOS/Linux launcher checks skipped), with 2,763 unit
tests, on the final runtime; an earlier full run before the last feature was
merged also passed 17 of 17. Hands-on checks of the candidate on Windows:

- The app from the release's CI build installed the game from a dump, updated an
  existing v1.1.2 installation (saves unchanged) and updated in place between two
  candidate builds.
- From the launcher, mods downloaded from Thunderstore, installed and enabled:
  HD Menus and UI (587 MB) and ZARF's Spanish translation; both were active in a
  match.
- The Portuguese (Brazil) mod, installed from Thunderstore through the launcher:
  translated menus and the fighter intro voice subtitles on screen, also together
  with a custom fighter (translated selector with the custom fighter in it).
- A three-outfit custom fighter (2Pac): selection art, all three outfits, final
  attributes and Charisma, face sculpt with neck normals, HD textures, the custom
  entrance, full One on One matches to the results screen, and Team selection
  with its own Team portrait and icons; the launcher enabled the pack and Play
  started the game with it.

**macOS (Apple Silicon)** and **Linux / Steam Deck** agents repeated the same checks on the
candidate, from this release's CI apps:

- macOS: full regression 16 of 21 on the first run; the five failures passed when rerun (three
  came from the test save, two are a rare start-up fault seen since v0.13 that did not recur in
  72 more boots). Install, update from v1.1.2 and between candidates, all Thunderstore mods,
  Portuguese subtitles on screen and the full 2Pac test passed; 2,749 unit tests.
- Steam Deck: full regression 20 of 21; only replay differs, the known
  [#98](https://github.com/vyanhursky/DefJamFFNY-recomp/issues/98). Install, both updates,
  Add to / Remove from Steam (on a test Steam folder), all Thunderstore mods, Portuguese
  subtitles on screen and the full 2Pac test passed.
- Neither saw the rare start-up crash
  [#285](https://github.com/vyanhursky/DefJamFFNY-recomp/issues/285) (1 in 13 starts on Windows
  with Portuguese, HD Menus and a custom fighter on together).

Created fighters whose HD textures decode to more than 192 MiB are refused
([#283](https://github.com/vyanhursky/DefJamFFNY-recomp/issues/283)).

Fighter Mod Support is new: not every fighter, animation or combination has been played, and
macOS, Linux and the Steam Deck have had less play than Windows. Keep a backup of the complete
data folder before enabling packs. Known issues carried forward:
[memory investigation #166](https://github.com/vyanhursky/DefJamFFNY-recomp/issues/166),
[Steam Deck replay differences #98](https://github.com/vyanhursky/DefJamFFNY-recomp/issues/98)
and the fighter follow-ups in [#247](https://github.com/vyanhursky/DefJamFFNY-recomp/issues/247).

Press F9 after a problem to create a local bug bundle; review it before sharing.
